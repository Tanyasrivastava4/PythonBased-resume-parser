1. Role Overview
We are seeking an experienced professional to lead Operational Excellence (OPEX), Business Process Reengineering (BPR)
and manufacturing transformation initiatives. The role will partner with client leadership, plant management and
cross-functional teams to assess existing operations, redesign processes, improve productivity, optimize costs and deliver
measurable, sustainable business outcomes.
The successful candidate should demonstrate ownership of end-to-end process transformation—from current-state
assessment and gap analysis through future-state design, implementation, change management and benefits realization.
2. Key Responsibilities
A. Business Process Reengineering and Process Transformation
• Lead end-to-end BPR initiatives across manufacturing and operational functions.
• Conduct AS-IS assessments, process mapping, gap analysis and root-cause identification.
• Design TO-BE processes, define improvement roadmaps and support implementation.
• Drive process standardization, change management, adoption and continuous improvement.
• Track outcomes and validate benefits achieved through process redesign.
B. Operational Excellence and Manufacturing Improvement
• Identify opportunities to improve productivity, reduce cost and waste, and optimize cycle time.
• Lead improvement initiatives across production, maintenance, quality, procurement and supply chain.
• Identify operational bottlenecks and implement sustainable corrective and preventive actions.
• Support plant performance improvement and manufacturing transformation programmes.
C. Lean Six Sigma and Structured Problem Solving
• Apply appropriate methods such as DMAIC, Value Stream Mapping (VSM), Kaizen, 5S, TPM, OEE, FMEA and Root Cause
Analysis (RCA).
• Facilitate structured problem-solving workshops and establish performance baselines.
• Monitor improvement actions and ensure benefits are measurable and sustained.
D. Project Delivery and Stakeholder Management
• Own project plans, milestones, deliverables, risks, action trackers and benefits realization.
• Coordinate with client leadership, plant teams and cross-functional stakeholders.
• Lead project reviews, drive accountability and support capability building and knowledge transfer.
• Prepare business cases, management presentations, dashboards and progress reports.
E. Performance Measurement and Business Impact
• Define and track KPIs for productivity, cost, quality, efficiency and cycle time.
• Quantify impact through cost savings, productivity gains, reduced downtime or other operational measures.
• Use data analysis, benchmarking and performance dashboards to identify further opportunities.
3. Mandatory Requirements
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Technology or a relevant discipline.

4. Preferred Experience and Skills

other industrial sectors.

5. Educational Qualification
• Mandatory: Bachelor’s degree in Engineering / Technology or a relevant discipline.
• Preferred: MBA / PGDM in Operations, Strategy, Supply Chain or a related specialization.
6. Deployment and Work Requirements
Potential deployment locations: South India, Odisha, Chhattisgarh, Telangana / Hyderabad and Rajkot.
The role may require travel to client and manufacturing plant locations, work on non-IT / shop-floor process improvement
projects, and fortnightly rotations depending on project requirements. Candidates should confirm their location flexibility and
willingness to travel or rotate as required.
7. Selection Criteria
Candidates will be assessed on end-to-end BPR ownership; AS-IS analysis and TO-BE design; implementation and change
management; manufacturing/shop-floor exposure; OPEX and Lean Six Sigma knowledge; leadership of cross-functional
projects; quantified business impact; client and senior stakeholder management; communication and analytical skills;
location/travel flexibility; notice period; and compensation expectations.
8. Information Required from Interested Candidates
• Updated CV.
• Current CTC, including fixed and variable components, and expected CTC.
• Notice period and earliest possible joining date.
• Current location and preferred work location(s).
• A brief example of an end-to-end BPR project personally led, covering the business problem, AS-IS assessment, TO-BE
redesign, implementation and measurable results.
• Confirmation of willingness to undertake a one-year contractual assignment and travel / work on fortnightly rotations, wherever
required.
Please share the requested details to support an initial assessment of your suitability for the role. Project allocation and deployment requirements will
