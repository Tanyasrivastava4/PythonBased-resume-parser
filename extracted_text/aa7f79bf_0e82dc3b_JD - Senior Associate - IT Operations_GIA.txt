GIA India Laboratory Pvt. Ltd.
Job Description
Job Title
: Senior Associate – IT Operations
Reports to
: Director IT Services – India
Location
: Mumbai (BKC)
JOB OVERVIEW
The position of Senior Associate is responsible for overseeing various aspects of IT Operations and coordinating with
stakeholders such as the Server and Network Administrator, End User Support team, and other departments to address
IT needs and requirements.
ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
• Assist the Network Administrator in maintaining and troubleshooting the organization’s data and voice network
infrastructure—across both on-premises and cloud environments—to ensure optimal performance and security of all
network components.
• Assist the System Administrator in managing Windows and Linux servers—across on-premises, cloud, and virtualized
environments (including Nutanix and VMware)—to ensure optimal performance and availability.
• Assist in delivering IT services to end users by resolving operational bottlenecks, maintaining regular communication
with stakeholders, and supporting the achievement of SLAs and TATs with business partners and vendors.
• Assist in managing the IT asset lifecycle, including budgeting, procurement, deployment, regular maintenance, and
secure disposals.
• Collaborate with the global Information Security team and internal IT groups to implement best practices, align local
processes with global security policies, and continuously enhance the organization’s security posture.
• Support various departmental IT operational needs by coordinating and assisting with ad hoc requirements such as
town halls, trainings, and other organizational events and needs
TECHNICAL COMPETENCIES (Knowledge, Skills & Abilities)
• Good understanding of networking protocols (TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP, etc.) and experience of working on switches,
routers, firewall and wireless devices.
• Good understanding and hands-on experience with Windows and Linux operating systems and virtualization platforms
like VMware or Nutanix. Knowledge of cloud platforms (Azure, AWS) is an advantage.
• Good understanding of ITIL frameworks.
• Familiarity with vulnerability scanning tools and incident response frameworks.
EDUCATION AND EXPERIENCE
• Any Bachelor's degree with 7-10 years of relevant experience.
• Certifications such as CCNA, AWS, Azure, Windows, Linux, ITIL will be an advantage. .
