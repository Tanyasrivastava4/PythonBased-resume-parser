process of converting their manual records and operations to digital
form. This includes digitizing their ledgers, accounts, loan records, and
other data. It also includes implementing digital systems for their
financial transactions, customer service, and other business
processes. The digitization of PACS is a major initiative in India, with
the government having allocated Rs. 2,516 crore for the project. The
goal is to digitize all 63,000 functional PACS in the country over a
period of five years. Benifits 1. Faster and more efficient loan
processing 2. Improved customer service 3. Increased transparency
and accountability 4. New opportunities for diversification
